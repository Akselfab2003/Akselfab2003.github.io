The ideaGoal

The goal was a bot that could trade on the stock market by itself, using information and indicators from the market as signals. Getting there meant learning the field properly, not just wiring up an API.

What I learnedConcepts
  • Backtesting — running a strategy against historical data before risking anything on it.
  • Sharpe ratio — judging returns relative to the risk taken to get them.
  • The Black–Scholes model — pricing options, as in the calculator above.
  • Trading methods across securities and equities, as well as options trading.
Risk & reliabilityPerspective
An adversarial environment

Markets are hostile input by design: every edge you find is one someone else is trying to take away. That makes a trading system a good teacher in testing assumptions instead of trusting them.
